**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

This policy explains what personal data ElevateHer360 collects through its website and participant mobile app, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It is written to meet the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda and its Regulations.

## 1. Who we are

ElevateHer360 is a learning, mentorship, career development and employment platform run by **Women in Technology Uganda (WitU)** ("we", "us", "our"). We decide how and why your personal data is used, so we are the data collector and data controller for it.

- **Registration with the Personal Data Protection Office:** [Registration number to be confirmed]
- **Privacy contact:** support@elevateher360.org

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the ElevateHer360 website (including the participant, mentor, employer, instructor and staff areas) and the ElevateHer360 participant app for Android and iOS. The app uses the same account and the same servers as the website.

## 3. Data we collect

**Account and sign-in details**

- Your name, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read them.
- Your account type and status, whether your email is verified, and when you last signed in.
- When you sign in to the app, a sign-in token for your device.

**Profile details you give us**

- Surname, given name and other names, and the branch or centre you belong to.
- Gender, date of birth, country, district and location.
- An optional profile photo.
- Education level, employment status, career interests and preferred language.
- Whether you have a disability and, if you choose to tell us, its type. This is sensitive (special) personal data. It is optional, and we use it only to give you accessibility support and to report on inclusion.

**Learning activity**

- Course applications and your answers, enrolments and cohorts.
- Lesson progress, quiz and assessment answers and scores, assignment submissions and the files you attach, grades, feedback, attendance and certificates.
- Library activity: books you read, your reading progress, bookmarks and download requests.

**Career, jobs and mentorship**

- Resumes and cover letters you build or upload, and the details in them.
- Jobs you save, applications you send (with any cover letter) and interview or offer records.
- Mentee and mentor profiles, mentor matches, mentoring sessions and the actions agreed in them.
- Goals you set, the steps you plan and your progress on them.

**AI Career Mentor**

- The questions you ask the AI Career Mentor, its answers, the answers you save and the steps you add to your calendar.

**Events, surveys and messages**

- Event registrations, attendance, feedback and event certificates.
- Survey answers, support requests and help-desk tickets.
- Notifications and announcements sent to you, and whether you have read them.

**Device and app data**

- A random identifier the app creates (not your phone's hardware ID), your push-notification token, your phone's platform (Android or iOS), the app version and whether notifications are on.
- Actions the app saves while you are offline, so they can be sent once you reconnect.

**Security records**

- Sign-in records: the email used, IP address, browser or device type, time, and why a sign-in failed.
- Records of important actions on the platform, with IP address and device type.
- Records of your agreement to this policy and our Terms of Use: the version, time and IP address.
- When AI tools are used, a record of which tool, which AI provider and whether it worked.

We do **not** collect your phone's location, contacts, call logs, SMS or advertising ID. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics tools.

## 4. Data stored on your phone

So the app works with a weak or no internet connection, it keeps some data on your phone:

- **Secure storage** (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain): your sign-in token and basic account details.
- **A local database:** copies of your courses, lessons, assignments, jobs, mentorship, events and notifications, and actions waiting to be sent.
- **Downloads:** lesson materials you choose to save for offline use.
- **Settings:** your app preferences.

**Fingerprint or face unlock:** if you turn it on, the app asks your phone to check your fingerprint or face. The check happens entirely on your phone. We never receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

When you sign out, the app deletes your sign-in token, cached data, waiting actions and downloads from the phone, and stops push notifications to it. Uninstalling the app also removes this data.

## 5. Why we use your data

- To create and run your account and keep it secure.
- To deliver learning: enrol you, track progress, receive and grade work, give feedback and issue certificates.
- To support your career: resumes, cover letters, job listings, and sending your applications to the employers you choose.
- To run mentorship: match you with a mentor and organise sessions and goals.
- To give you AI career guidance when you ask for it (see section 7).
- To send you notifications, push messages and emails about your account, courses, deadlines, events and opportunities.
- To make the platform accessible to you.
- To prevent misuse and fraud and keep records of important actions.
- To measure and improve our programmes, using figures that do not identify you wherever possible.
- To meet our legal obligations.

## 6. Our legal basis

We use your personal data on these grounds under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019:

- **Your consent.** When you register, you agree to this policy and our Terms of Use, and we record that. Optional and sensitive information, such as disability status, is used only with your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.
- **Providing the programme you joined.** Most account, learning, career and mentorship data is needed to deliver it.
- **Our legitimate interests.** Keeping the platform secure and improving our programmes, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligations.** Where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Push notifications only appear if you allow them on your phone. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

## 7. Who we share data with

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only as follows:

- **Our staff, instructors and mentors:** only what their role needs, for example an instructor sees submissions for their course and a mentor sees their mentees' mentorship details. Access is controlled by roles and permissions.
- **Employers on the platform:** your application, resume, cover letter and the profile details in them, **only when you apply** to their job. After that, the employer is responsible for how it uses them.
- **AI providers (OpenAI, Google or Anthropic, depending on which we have switched on):**
    - When you use the **AI Career Mentor**, we send your question, the earlier messages in that conversation, and this context so the advice fits you: your name, education level, employment status, career interests, district and country, your goals and progress, and the titles, dates and agreed actions of your mentorship sessions (including your mentor's name).
    - When you use the **resume or cover letter tools**, we send the resume content and job details you submit.
    - Nothing is sent unless you use one of these tools.
- **Google Firebase and Apple:** your push token and the content of notifications sent to you, to deliver push notifications.
- **Our email provider:** your name, email address and the message, to send emails.
- **Our hosting providers:** all platform data, stored on our behalf.
- **Authorities, courts or legal advisers:** only what the law requires, or what is needed to protect the safety and rights of our users and platform.

When we report programme results to partners or funders, we use figures that do not identify you, unless you have agreed otherwise.

## 8. Data sent outside Uganda

Some providers above, including Google, Apple and the AI providers, may store or process data on servers outside Uganda. As the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 requires, we only do this where the provider's country or the provider itself protects personal data at least as well as Ugandan law, or where you have agreed to it.

## 9. How long we keep data

- Account, profile, learning, career and mentorship data: while your account is active, and deleted or de-identified when your account is deleted (see section 12).
- Push tokens: removed when you sign out of the app or delete your account.
- Data on your phone: until you sign out or uninstall the app.
- Security, audit and consent records: longer where needed to protect the platform, settle disputes or meet legal obligations, de-identified where possible.
- Statistics that do not identify you may be kept for programme reporting.

## 10. How we protect your data

- All data between the app or browser and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Passwords are hashed, and the app keeps its sign-in token in the phone's secure storage.
- Access inside the platform is limited by roles and permissions, and important actions are logged.
- Uploaded files, such as assignments and CVs, are kept in private storage and are only shown to signed-in users who are allowed to see them. The digital library is only available to signed-in members.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will tell the Personal Data Protection Office and, where required, you. If you think your account has been misused, change your password and email support@elevateher360.org straight away.

## 11. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
- **Correct** data that is wrong or incomplete. You can update most profile details yourself.
- **Delete** your account and personal data (see section 12).
- **Withdraw consent** for anything that relies on your consent, including sensitive information.
- **Object to or stop** certain uses of your data, such as uses based on our legitimate interests.
- **Not be subject to decisions made only by automated means** that significantly affect you. The AI Career Mentor only gives advice; it does not make decisions about you.
- **Complain** to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) at www.pdpo.go.ug.

To use these rights, email **support@elevateher360.org** from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.

## 12. Deleting your account and data

**How to ask for deletion**

- **In the app:** open **Profile → Settings → Delete my account**.
- **On the website:** sign in and use the form at [site.elevateher360.org/account-deletion](https://site.elevateher360.org/account-deletion).
- **By email:** if you cannot sign in, email support@elevateher360.org from your account's email address with the subject "Account deletion request".

You do not need the app installed to ask for deletion. We review each request and complete it within **30 days**.

**What we delete**

- Your name, email, phone number, password and app sign-in tokens.
- Your profile, including photo, gender, date of birth, location, disability information, education, employment and career interests.
- Push tokens, device records and notifications addressed to you.
- Resumes, uploaded CVs, cover letters, saved jobs and personal goals.
- Your mentee profile, AI Career Mentor conversations, saved answers and planned steps.
- Library bookmarks, reading history and download requests.

**What we keep, without your identity**

- Programme records such as enrolments, lesson progress, assessment results, submissions, certificates, attendance, event registrations, survey and feedback answers, mentorship sessions and job applications. These are kept for programme reporting but are no longer linked to your name or contact details.
- Records the law requires us to keep, only as long as needed.
- Security logs (de-identified where possible) and statistics that do not identify you.
- Copies of applications you already sent to an employer, which that employer holds. Contact the employer to have those deleted.

Deletion is permanent: you will no longer be able to sign in, and certificates can no longer be linked to you through the platform. Download anything you want to keep first.

Signing out of the app or uninstalling it removes data from your phone but **does not** delete your account. To delete your account, use one of the options above.

## 13. Cookies and browser storage

The website uses only the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and a security token that protects forms. It also saves your accessibility choices (such as text size or high contrast) in your browser. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

## 14. Children

ElevateHer360 is for people aged **18 and over**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you think this has happened, email support@elevateher360.org.

We may update this policy when the platform or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make significant changes, we will tell you through the platform, the app or by email, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
